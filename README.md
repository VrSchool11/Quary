# Quary
A fun 2d retro game where you have been kidnapped and your limbs have been cut off and you must collect limbs found on the ground or kill the guards for their limbs your goal is to escape and kill the final boss who is the king.  

Links:
Github Repo: https://github.com/VrSchool11/Quary
Playable Website: 

Controls:

A D = move
Space = jump, if you have a leg that can
J = strike, from behind it is quiet
K = fire, if you took a gun and kept a hand to hold it
E hold = fit the limb you are standing on
C hold = build a can for the gun out of the three parts
Q = which side the limb goes on, once both sockets are full
M = sound on and off
Esc = pause

Known Problems:

* You dont have to kill the guards you can just run

How to Play:

You must escape the compound that you are being held captive in and in order to do so you must go past the guards, while you were taken your limbs have been cut off and you must escape the compound with the help of the limbs that you steal from the guards and animals. On the top you have a bar with tell you how human you look an increased humanity bar means that gaurd takes longer to spot you and a low one means guards spot you quicker you also have the ability to steal guns and ammo you can also craft a silencer from the parts, your end goal is to kill the king who is the final boss and escape the compound
