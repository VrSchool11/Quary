Quary
A fun 2d retro game where you have been kidnapped and your limbs have been cut off and you must collect limbs found on the ground or kill the guards for their limbs, your goal is to escape and kill the final boss who is the king.

Links: Github Repo: https://github.com/VrSchool11/Quary Playable Website: https://vrschool11.github.io/Quary/

Controls:

A D = move Space = jump, if you have a leg that can J = strike, from behind it is quiet K = fire, if you took a gun and kept a hand to hold it E hold = fit the limb you are standing on C hold = build a can for the gun out of the three parts Q = which side the limb goes on, once both sockets are full M = sound on and off Esc = pause

Arrow keys do the same as A D and Space. Any key skips the text cards, but the zone card runs on its own timer so it does not wait for you.

How to Play:

You must escape the compound that you are being held captive in and to do that you have to get past five zones of guards, while you were taken your limbs were cut off so you start on one leg and you cannot really move until you take a leg off something dead and fit it on by holding E. You do the same with arms and with your second leg and you keep swapping parts the whole way through, so what you are wearing at the end is whatever you picked up on the way.

Humanity:

At the top you have a bar that tells you how human you look, it is just the four limbs you are wearing added up and nothing else. A high bar means the guards take longer to spot you and a low bar means they see you from far off and shoot on sight. Human limbs give you humanity and animal limbs take it away, and the animal limbs are all lying on the open ground where you cannot miss them while the human ones are up on the ledges, so the easy parts are the ones that turn you into a monster. Standing in the ferns hides you completely, the floodlights in the yard let the guards see about twice as far, and if you leave a zone without being seen once you get some health back.

The Limbs:

Legs decide how high you jump, how fast you move and how much noise you make. A jaguar leg jumps really high and is almost silent but wrecks your humanity, a crocodile leg is quiet but slow and low to the ground, human legs sit in the middle. If you are down to one leg you can only hop and you are slow and loud. Arms decide your damage and your reach in a fight, and only human arms and the officer arm can actually hold a gun, the animal arms hit harder but you cannot shoot with them on.

The Gun:

The first guard you kill drops his sidearm, after that guards drop spare mags and a mag is five rounds. Firing it bare wakes up the whole zone and everyone comes for you. There are three parts hidden up on the ledges, a sleeve, a spring and a rag, and once you have all three you hold C to build a can for the gun so the shots go quiet. You still need a human hand to fire it at all.

The Zones:

There are five zones and each one has its own rule. The block has no cover so you learn the patrol loops and go when they turn, undergrowth is full of ferns so you can sneak most of it, the yard is lit so they see you coming from far away, the ridge is a straight climb so your legs decide if you make the jumps, and the treeline is where the king is waiting at the fence. There are notes to read scattered around every zone.

Enemies:

Guards walk a set path. A commander runs for a radio if he spots you and if he reaches it the whole zone goes hot and stays that way, so take him out before he sees you. The king's own is the big armoured one, slow but hits hard. Jaguars are fast and do not care which way they are facing. Monkeys just run away. The king has thirty health and three phases and he only opens up mid swing, and he is wearing the leg they cut off you, which is worth the most humanity in the game and is also the worst leg in the game.

Health:

You have six health. If you die inside a zone you can carry on from the start of that zone with three health and everything you were carrying. Leaving a zone without being spotted heals you back up a bit. Standing around in a zone too long brings extra hunters in from the fence.
